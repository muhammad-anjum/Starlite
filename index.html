The Starlite feeling

Care that feels like a second home.

Starlite Childcare is a licensed, well-maintained home daycare serving families in East Dublin. For more than 15 years, Ayesha Javed has created a safe, nurturing space where children can settle in, explore, and build confidence.

The days are shaped around each child’s stage, with educational activities that encourage creativity and kindergarten readiness.
